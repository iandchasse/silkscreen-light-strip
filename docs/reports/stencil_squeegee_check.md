Method: every extrusion segment is swept by its `;WIDTH` and unioned per layer (as in `slice_check.py`); the plate's position is read from the first-layer beads of the two runner rails. The model is sectioned at every layer's middle and compared with the sliced layer. The slot, slit and hole gaps are the bead-free stretch through the feature's centre line.
